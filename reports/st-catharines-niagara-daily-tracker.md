## 1) Source-selection framework (how sources were screened)

I screened sources using a weighted filter so the daily brief is not just a random news summary.

### Screening criteria (100-point model)
- **Policy/price transmission strength (30 pts):** How directly the source can affect mortgage cost, housing supply, tax burden, or demand.
- **Geographic relevance (25 pts):** Niagara/St. Catharines specificity.
- **Update frequency (20 pts):** Daily/weekly cadence suitable for monitoring.
- **Data authority (15 pts):** Official/regulatory/industry source quality.
- **Actionability (10 pts):** Whether signals can be translated into buy/sell/wait implications.

## 2) Collection log (what was actually checked)

> This section is intentionally explicit so you can audit what was reviewed versus inferred.

### A) Bank of Canada
- **URL checked:** https://www.bankofcanada.ca/core-functions/monetary-policy/key-interest-rate/
- **What was observed:** The page is the official policy-rate anchor and primary source for rate path communication.
- **Market channel:** Mortgage qualification/payment sensitivity.
- **Expected housing impact channel:** Lower/less restrictive rate expectations typically improve buyer purchasing power and turnover.

### B) Niagara Region News Releases
- **URL checked:** https://www.niagararegion.ca/news/default.aspx
- **Items observed on page:**
  - “Niagara Region approves 2026 Budget” (Feb 13, 2026)
  - “NRH Board of Directors elects 2026 officers with St. Catharines Mayor Mat Siscoe as Chair” (Jan 28, 2026)
  - “Niagara Region approves Waste Management and Water and Wastewater 2026 budgets”
- **Market channel:** Taxes/fees/services/infrastructure timing.
- **Expected housing impact channel:** Budget and utility decisions can affect household carrying costs, developer cost stacks, and long-run valuation support.

### C) Ontario Newsroom
- **URL checked:** https://news.ontario.ca/en
- **What was observed:** Active stream of provincial announcements/advisories.
- **Market channel:** Province-level housing supply policy, approvals framework, and economic confidence.
- **Expected housing impact channel:** Policy changes generally impact prices with a lag through supply approvals and migration/employment conditions.

### D) City of St. Catharines planning and permits pages
- **URLs checked:**
  - https://www.stcatharines.ca/en/planning-and-development/development-applications.aspx
  - https://www.stcatharines.ca/en/building-and-renovating/issued-permits.aspx
- **What was observed:** Development/permit pages are available and usable as local supply pipeline indicators.
- **Market channel:** Future inventory pipeline.
- **Expected housing impact channel:** Faster permitting and approvals can reduce medium-term supply pressure.

### E) CREA media/news
- **URL checked:** https://www.crea.ca/news/
- **What was observed:** Official CREA media hub for national housing commentary and releases.
- **Market channel:** Momentum/sentiment baseline for Ontario and spillover to Niagara demand behavior.
- **Expected housing impact channel:** Useful for identifying turns in activity before local monthly data stabilizes.

### F) Statistics Canada (context layer)
- **URL checked:** https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/dai-quo/index-eng.htm
- **What was observed:** Daily statistical release bulletin (macro context).
- **Market channel:** Inflation/labor/population trends affecting rates and household demand.

## 4) Why these policy/news categories are important

1. **Interest-rate policy:** strongest short-cycle affordability lever.
2. **Regional budgets/utilities:** direct effect on carrying cost assumptions and new-build economics.
3. **Provincial housing framework:** controls approval speed, supply feasibility, and confidence.
4. **Local permits/development applications:** practical forward indicator of supply.
5. **National market releases:** useful for turning-point detection and behavioral confirmation.

## 5) Daily operating procedure (repeatable workflow)

### Step-by-step routine (30–40 min/day)
1. Check BoC policy-rate and latest press release page.
2. Check Niagara Region News Releases for budget/utility/infrastructure items.
3. Check Ontario Newsroom for housing/municipal/planning announcements.
4. Check St. Catharines development applications + issued permits pages.
5. Check CREA and Statistics Canada for macro confirmation.
6. Score top 3 signals and publish one-paragraph market stance.

### Output format for each day
- **Top signals (3–5)**
- **Impact path (demand/supply/financing/expectations)**
- **Bias for local prices (1–3 months, 6–12 months)**
- **Confidence level + what to watch next**

## 6) Current limitations and mitigation

- Some media pages are dynamic and less stable for automated extraction.
- To avoid noise, this brief prioritizes official policy/regulatory/government channels first, then adds media context only as a secondary layer.
- For stronger investment decisions, pair this brief with local monthly board-level transaction/inventory statistics.
